Icon-only button for compact actions (back-to-top, social, toolbar).

```jsx
<IconButton variant="solid" label="Yukarı çık"><ChevronUpIcon /></IconButton>
<IconButton variant="soft" label="LinkedIn"><LinkedinIcon /></IconButton>
```

Variants: `solid` (orange) · `soft` (orange-50 tint) · `ghost`. Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Always provide `label`.
